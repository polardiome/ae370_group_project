The satellite's acceleration in the x-direction is:

$$
\ddot{x} =
-\frac{\mu x}{(x^2+y^2)^{3/2}}
-\frac{C_D A \rho(h)}{2m}
v_{\mathrm{rel}}v_{\mathrm{rel},x}
+u_x(t)
$$

The satellite's acceleration in the y-direction is:

$$
\ddot{y} =
-\frac{\mu y}{(x^2+y^2)^{3/2}}
-\frac{C_D A \rho(h)}{2m}
v_{\mathrm{rel}}v_{\mathrm{rel},y}
+u_y(t)
$$

### 2. Vector Form of the Equations of Motion

The combined vector equation is:

$$
\boxed{
\ddot{\mathbf{r}} =
-\frac{\mu}{r^3}\mathbf{r}
-\frac{C_D A \rho(h)}{2m}
\left|\mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{rel}}\right|
\mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{rel}}
+\mathbf{u}(t)
}
$$
